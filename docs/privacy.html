Triton Fuel Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

The short version

Triton Fuel doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you log stays on your own phone.

What stays on your device

The meals you log, your daily history and your goals are saved only in the app's storage on your phone. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app downloads

When it opens, the app downloads the day's dining hall menu (a public file hosted on GitHub Pages at refreshrefreshclan-lang.github.io). This request doesn't include anything about you or your logs. Like any website, GitHub may record basic technical details such as your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Where the nutrition information comes from

Menus and nutrition facts come from UC San Diego Housing, Dining and Hospitality's public menu pages. Values can change on the day, so treat them as close estimates, not medical advice.

Triton Fuel is an independent student project. It is not made, endorsed or supported by UC San Diego.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/refreshrefreshclan-lang/triton-fuel/issues.
